Literally shared code (dp_core/model.py): the image encoder (ResNet18, GroupNorm, spatial softmax, lines 70-90), the observation tokens (157-164), the transformer encoder and decoder with the Linear 256 to 8 head (93-125), the data, optimiser, EMA and checkpoint format. FlowPolicy is a subclass that overrides only two methods, not shared: the loss and the sampler (flow.py 19-35 against model.py 166-198). They also differ in what the time input means (an integer 0 to 99 where 99 is noise, against t x 100 with t = 0 as noise) and in the noise schedule (cosine alphas against a linear blend). The head, the normalisation and the optimiser are identical; the unused alpha table is still stored in flow checkpoints.

How each model samples an action chunk, over the real rollout

one video per task (lift, drawer, stack, pour), diffusion first, then flow matching

Every line is the gripper path the simulator produces for one of 32 sampled chunks (the recorded state is restored and the chunk applied; forward kinematics, projected into the camera), smoothed for display through the real 16 points. Each attempt pauses once, at the call whose 16-step chunk end gets closest to the marker (cube, handle, cube 2) before the grasp, and replays the 10 sampling steps; it is cut 1 s after success. A chunk is only 16 steps (lift 0.32 s, drawer 0.53 s, stack 0.80 s), so it ends part-way to the marker, never at the target. Pour differs: that env has no water particles, the lines are the bottle-mouth path of each sampled chunk (simulator-restored), the markers are the cup opening (yellow) and the pour region of the env (green), the replay is the call that contains the start of the tilt (call 14, step 208, both models), chunks are 32 steps with 16 executed, and the flow clip has no velocity arrows. Other lift versions: lift with several replays per attempt (diffusion, flow) and with a single replay (diffusion, flow); details in media/README_task_videos.md.

Flow matching: how it learns

Best for flow matching: pour 57.0 % (5 steps), lift 78.2 % (10 steps)
Training: batch 128, AdamW (learning rate 1e-4, 500 warm-up steps, cosine decay, weight decay 1e-4), gradient clip 1.0, bf16, EMA 0.999 weights evaluated, 30,000 steps (identical for both). Sizes come from the checkpoints and code (dp_core/model.py, flow.py, train_dp.py): 128 × 128 workspace and wrist images side by side, 2 past frames, 16 steps predicted and 8 executed, width 256, 100 training timesteps with a cosine schedule. Pouring: two arms (7 + 7 action values), 32 steps predicted and 16 executed, 5 sampling steps, 80,000 training steps. The five small plots per method are a real demo chunk (drawer demos, one joint) corrupted with the real schedule; the sampling paths are schematic (one coordinate, a perfect network). Success rates: held-out seeds, 200 episodes per seed; lift seed-1001 step sweeps from the report.
